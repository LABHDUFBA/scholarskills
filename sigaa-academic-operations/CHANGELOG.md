# Changelog

## [0.1.0] — 2026-11-26

### Added

- Generic SIGAA-like academic operations protocol.
- Human approval gate for persistent actions and formal submissions.
- Explicit handling of e-mail notifications and read-back verification.
- Offline request checker with positive, negative and edge-case tests.
- Compatibility guidance for institution-specific adapters.

### Known limitations

- No live browser integration is shipped.
- No institutional adapter is bundled in the generic package.
- The checker validates protocol shape, not academic or scientific truth.
