Executable research methodologies that can be read, tested and cited.

Maturity levels — evidence, not aspiration

A level is granted only with the artefact that justifies it, stored in the skill repository. Green tests prove structure; they do not prove a method. Peer review and replication are social processes with their own records.

Experimental

Works on the author's machine. No independent evidence.

Tested

Automated tests pass in a recorded environment (tests/RESULTS.md).

Peer-reviewed

Two named reviewers applied the editorial policy; verdicts in reviews/.

Replicated

Re-executed by a non-author with a published report.
