About ScholarSkills

Scope, team, governance, licences, accessibility and how to cite the project.

Scope

ScholarSkills is an open academic repository of skills for AI agents, maintained by the Digital Humanities Laboratory of the Federal University of Bahia (LABHDUFBA) within the Métodos Digitais initiative. It gathers packages that operationalise research procedures or research-support activities, with explicit documentation, tests, limits and authorship.

The catalogue is neither a prompt directory nor a plugin store. Inclusion does not imply peer review, replication or institutional endorsement of a package's results.

Team

Coordination and co-authorship are named in the authorship section of the home page, with affiliation and ORCID when provided. Each package page states its own authorship; not every person in the project signs every skill.

Governance and editorial policy

The editorial policy defines what a submission must contain, the evidence dimensions, the review criteria and the correction and withdrawal procedure. Two people review each submission and record a verdict; the current policy is preserved until the maintainers formally revise it. Human review is not replaced by an LLM.

DOI deposits require explicit authorisation from the maintainers. A change on the main branch does not update an archived Zenodo record.

How to cite

For the catalogue, use the repository's CITATION.cff and the project's concept DOI. For a specific skill, use the version DOI shown on the package page where one exists. The concept DOI identifies the record that gathers the versions; the version DOI identifies the run you used.

Licences and rights

The project's original code and documentation use MIT, per the root LICENSE and each package's licence file. Third-party fonts, logos, trademarks and images may carry their own terms; presence in this repository does not grant MIT rights over them. External data keeps its source licence.

Accessibility

The portal targets WCAG 2.2 level AA as an engineering goal: contrast, visible focus, keyboard navigation, alternative text, reflow and respect for reduced motion. This is not a certification: no external accessibility audit has been carried out. Problems can be reported through the contact below.

Contact

To propose a skill, report a problem or ask for clarification: labhdufba@gmail.com. The repository is at github.com/LABHDUFBA/scholarskills.

History and transparency

Earlier implementation and deposit decisions are recorded in the repository's DEPLOYMENT_LOG.md; older entries describe past states. The portal uses no tracking, cookies or telemetry. The machine-readable catalogue is at registry.json.
