DOU and Federal Covenants for Research Pipelines

Collect official gazette publications and federal covenants through the state's own machine-readable routes, with pagination that stops at an empty answer and a filter that is verified before any batch.

What it solves

Collect DOU publications and federal covenants through official open-data routes, without circumventing access controls and without confusing silence from the source with absence of publication.

Limits

The gazette is published under the access-to-information law and its decree; the client does not circumvent blocks, rotate identities or use third-party proxies. Acts naming individuals are public, but building a database of people is personal-data processing under the LGPD. The limit is one request per second and 40 pages per run. The record proves what the route returned on the day; for legal effect the certified copy prevails.
