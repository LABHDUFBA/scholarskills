DOU and Federal Covenants for Research Pipelines

Collect official gazette publications and federal covenants through the state's own machine-readable routes, with pagination that stops at an empty answer and a filter that is verified before any batch.

Portal da Transparência API key

RequiredcredentialPORTAL_TRANSPARENCIA_KEY

What it is: Free key that authorises covenant queries through the official API.

How to get it: Sign up at portaldatransparencia.gov.br, open 'API de Dados' and generate a key. It travels in the chave-api-dados header.

export PORTAL_TRANSPARENCIA_KEY="sua_chave_aqui"
Generate a key ↗

Careful: Not needed to parse a saved search page: the parser works offline.

Objective

Collect DOU publications and federal covenants through official open-data routes, without circumventing access controls and without confusing silence from the source with absence of publication.
