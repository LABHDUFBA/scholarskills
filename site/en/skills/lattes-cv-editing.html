Lattes CV Editing for Academic Records

Prepare and check items before typing them into a Lattes CV: required fields per type, duplicate detection by normalised title and year, and the rule that a preprint is not a published article.

SS-0013 Tested Available Writing and publishing v1.0.0· MIT (code) / CC-BY-4.0 (docs)
PythonHermes Agent

Requirements

What you must have before running this skill. Everything required appears here — credentials included.

Python 3.10 or newer

Requiredsoftware

What it is: To run the item checker.

How to get it: Check with python3 --version.

Download Python ↗

Access to your own Lattes profile

Requiredsetting

What it is: The editor requires the CV holder's own login. The skill stores no password and never receives one.

How to get it: Sign in to the CNPq editor with your gov.br account. Editing and submission remain your decision.

Open the Lattes editor ↗

Careful: Permission to edit is not permission to submit to CNPq: they are different authorisations.

Objective

Prepare and check items before typing them into a Lattes CV, where no validator will catch a wrong year or a duplicated record.

Features

Duplicate by title and yearPreprint ≠ articleApproval gate
