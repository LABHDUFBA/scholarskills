Lattes CV Editing for Academic Records

Prepare and check items before typing them into a Lattes CV: required fields per type, duplicate detection by normalised title and year, and the rule that a preprint is not a published article.

SS-0013 Research infrastructure Tested Available Writing and publishing v1.0.0· MIT

PythonHermes Agent

What it solves

Prepare and check items before typing them into a Lattes CV, where no validator will catch a wrong year or a duplicated record.

Limits

The skill operates only the holder's own CV, with no scraping of third-party CVs and no automated submission. A field without a primary source stays empty. The CV is public and personal at once: publish only what the holder approved. The checker verifies structure and consistency, not the truth of the record.
