Métodos Digitais initiative

ScholarSkills

Scientific skills for artificial intelligence agents

An open academic repository of research protocols, procedures and resources organised for use by AI agents. Published packages state their requirements, procedure, evidence, limits, authorship and conditions of reuse.

The initiative seeks to broaden the transparency and reuse of AI-assisted research procedures while preserving human scientific responsibility. Not every skill is validated, replicated or has a DOI: each record states what it has.

Four words the catalogue assumes

Model, agent, skill and tool are not synonyms. The difference is what gives meaning to what is published here.

Model

The engine that interprets and generates language. On its own it keeps no record and performs no action.

Agent

The system that coordinates a cycle of decision, action and verification, with tools and state.

Skill

The versioned package that guides the agent through a bounded task, with criteria, tests and limits.

Tool

The API, program or database that actually performs the operation.
